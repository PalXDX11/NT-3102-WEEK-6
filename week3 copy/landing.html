About Sword Art Online

Sword Art Online follows Kirito, a player trapped in a virtual reality game with thousands of players. They must clear all 100 floors of the game's castle to escape. If they die in the game, they also die in real life.

Players must work together and overcome obstacles to survive and reach their goals.
